Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Blankleaf works and where your notes are kept.

Is Blankleaf free?

Yes. Blankleaf is free to use, with no account, no sign-up and no paid plan.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and start typing. Your notes are saved automatically in your browser.

Where are my notes saved?

In your own browser, on your own device. Blankleaf has no server that stores notes, so nothing you write is uploaded.

Can anyone else read my notes?

Your notes stay on your device unless you choose to share one. A shared note is encrypted end to end and goes directly from your browser to the person you send the link to.

Will I lose my notes if I close the tab?

No. Notes are saved as you type. Each visit opens on a blank page, and your earlier notes are waiting in the Notes list in the same browser.

How could I lose my notes?

Clearing your browser’s site data deletes them, and they only exist in the browser where you wrote them. Export a backup now and then from Settings, under Data.

Do my notes sync between devices?

No, notes stay on the device where you wrote them. To move them, export a backup file and import it on the other device, or share a single note by link.

Does Blankleaf work offline?

Yes. After your first visit it opens and saves without a connection. You can also install it from your browser so it opens like an app.

Can I get back an earlier version of a note?

Yes. Blankleaf saves versions every few minutes while you write and keeps the latest 50 for each note on your device. Open Version history to read one and restore it.

What happens to notes I delete?

They move to Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days. After that they are deleted permanently.

How does sharing a note work?

Create a link, choose when it expires, and optionally add a password. Keep Blankleaf open until the link has been opened, because the note is sent straight from your browser.

Does Blankleaf work on phones?

Yes. On a phone you get a layout designed for small screens, with the same notes, formats and tools.
